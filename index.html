🎯 UPSC Prelims Practice

Practice multiple-choice questions and strengthen your conceptual understanding.

Sample Question - Indian Polity

Which part of the Indian Constitution contains the Fundamental Rights?

A. Part II

B. Part III

C. Part IV

D. Part V

Correct Answer: B. Part III

Explanation: Articles 12 to 35 of the Indian Constitution deal with Fundamental Rights.

UPSC Trick: Fundamental Rights = Part III.
